# Nexus AI Workforce: Step-by-Step Master Tutorial

This tutorial will guide you from a fresh installation to a fully operational AI workforce. Follow these steps to transform your WordPress site into an AI-powered command center.

---

## Phase 1: Infrastructure Setup (The Skeleton)

### Step 1: Secure Your Engine
**Action:** Navigate to **Nexus AI > System Config**.
1. Enter your **OpenAI**, **Claude**, or **Gemini** API keys.
2. Click **Encrypt & Save**.
**What to expect:**
- The "Health Diagnostics" panel should show a green "System Secure" badge.
- Your keys are now encrypted with AES-256-CTR, protected even if your database is compromised.

### Step 2: Brand Your Command Center
**Action:** Scroll down to **Agency White Label**.
1. Upload your logo and select your primary brand color.
2. Save changes.
**What to expect:**
- The dashboard UI accents will instantly change to your brand color.
- If you are an agency, the "Nexus AI" name will be replaced by your chosen brand in client portals.

---

## Phase 2: Knowledge Ingestion (The Brain)

### Step 3: Feed the Company Brain
**Action:** Go to **Nexus AI > Company Brain**.
1. Upload your Company SOPs (PDF), Product Catalog (CSV), and Brand Voice guidelines.
2. Enter your website URL in the **URL Scraper** and click **Index**.
**What to expect:**
- You will see a progress bar as the system chunks and indexes your data.
- Once complete, the "Memory Integrity" panel will update with your total indexed documents.
- **Result:** Every agent you hire later will now have access to this shared intelligence.

---

## Phase 3: Workforce Deployment (The Talent)

### Step 4: Hire Your Executive Team
**Action:** Go to **Nexus AI > Hire AI Agents**.
1. **CEO:** Hire a CEO agent with a "Visionary" communication style and high "KPI focus."
2. **CMO:** Hire a CMO agent powered by **Claude 3.5** for superior creative writing.
3. **Developer:** Hire a Developer agent with **Temperature 0.2** for precise, logic-heavy code.
**What to expect:**
- A "Deployment Successful" notification.
- Your agents will appear in the "Active Workforce" list, ready for orders.

---

## Phase 4: Collaboration & Execution (The Output)

### Step 5: Start a Strategy Meeting
**Action:** Open the **Nexus Chat** (Overview or Sidebar).
1. Click **Start Meeting**.
2. Invite your CEO, CMO, and CFO agents.
3. Topic: "Analyze our Q3 performance and suggest a Q4 scaling plan."
**What to expect:**
- Real-time interaction as agents debate. The CMO suggests ads, the CFO warns about budget, and the CEO synthesizes the final plan.
- At the end, you receive "Meeting Minutes" in Markdown format.

### Step 6: Launch an Automation
**Action:** Go to **Nexus AI > Automations**.
1. Select the **Content Machine** blueprint.
2. Assign your "Research Agent" and "Copywriter Agent."
3. Click **Run Workflow**.
**What to expect:**
- The system will automatically execute the sequence.
- **Final Result:** A high-quality, SEO-optimized draft post will appear in your WordPress **Posts > Drafts** folder.

---

5. **Automated Processes** that create real value in your WordPress site.
